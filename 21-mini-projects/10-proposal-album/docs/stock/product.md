# Proposal album product design

THE DAY presents the same sample memories in 17 interactive album styles. The style picker lists all styles, including the default Chapter Cinema. A `?demo=1` through `?demo=17` URL opens the selected style directly.

- Chapter Cinema (16) plays memories by chapter. Viewers can add chapters and episodes, import local photos, and adjust captions, order, duration, and photo layout before playing.
- Confession Cinema (17) advances one sentence or scene at a time. Viewers can edit and rehearse scenes. The final confession opens through its dedicated button.

Edits and imported photos last until the viewer refreshes or switches styles. The imported photos stay in the browser. The album has no permanent storage or export.
